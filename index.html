Salutations to Lord Rama, the auspicious one, luminous as the moon,
the Lord of the Raghu dynasty, the protector, and the beloved of Sita.

Heartfelt Wishes to You & Your Family

May the divine blessings of Lord Sri Rama fill your life with joy, peace, and prosperity.

May Mata Sita's grace bring love and harmony to your home, Lakshmana's devotion inspire unwavering loyalty in all your bonds, and Hanuman's strength give you the courage to overcome every obstacle.

May this sacred festival of Sri Rama Navami illuminate your path with the eternal light of Dharma and Righteousness.

Jai Shri Ram
